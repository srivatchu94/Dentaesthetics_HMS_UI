# DHANTHA - THE DENTAL COMPANY
## Logo Design Specification & Brand Guidelines

---

## 🎨 LOGO CONCEPT

**Name Meaning**: Dhantha (धंत) - The trunk of an elephant in Sanskrit, symbolizing wisdom, strength, and precision

**Design Philosophy**: 
- Combines the elegance and strength of an elephant's trunk with modern dental aesthetics
- The trunk curves naturally, forming the shape of a pristine tooth
- Elephant symbolism represents: Trustworthiness, Memory, Wisdom, Longevity
- Tooth representation emphasizes: Health, Precision, Care, Cleanliness

## 📐 DESIGN ELEMENTS BREAKDOWN

### 1. **Elephant Trunk Component**
- **Shape**: Graceful curve representing strength and elegance
- **Gradient**: Linear gradient from teal (#2E7D8C) to dark teal (#1A5A66)
- **Direction**: Top-left to bottom-right for visual depth
- **Stroke**: Subtle 1.5px outline (#1A5A66)
- **Shadow**: Drop shadow (dx=0, dy=2, blur=3) for dimension

### 2. **Tooth Elements** (3 stylized teeth)
- **Top Tooth**: Central position, largest, most prominent
- **Right Tooth**: Supporting angle, dynamic placement
- **Left Tooth**: Balanced composition
- **Fill**: Pearl white with subtle sky-blue stroke
- **Inner Highlight**: Light fill (#F5FFFE) at 80% opacity for shine

### 3. **Elephant Eye**
- **Position**: Integrated into trunk curve
- **Color**: Dark teal (#1A5A66) with white pupil highlight
- **Purpose**: Adds personality and wildlife connection
- **Size**: 4px radius with subtle shine

### 4. **Background Circle**
- **Color**: Light blue background (#F0F8FA)
- **Border**: 2px teal stroke (#2E7D8C)
- **Effect**: Creates contained, professional frame
- **Shadow**: Soft drop shadow for elevation

### 5. **Typography**
- **Primary Text**: "DHANTHA"
  - Font: Segoe UI / Arial Bold
  - Size: 28px
  - Weight: Bold (700)
  - Color: #1A5A66
  - Letter Spacing: 1px
  - Effect: Stands out prominently

- **Tagline**: "THE DENTAL COMPANY"
  - Font: Segoe UI / Arial Regular
  - Size: 11px
  - Weight: Regular (400)
  - Color: #2E7D8C
  - Italic: Yes
  - Letter Spacing: 0.5px

- **Brand Promise**: "Nature of Elegance"
  - Font: Segoe UI / Arial Regular
  - Size: 9px
  - Weight: Regular (400)
  - Style Italic
  - Color: #4A9FB8 with 80% opacity
  - Letter Spacing: 0.3px

### 6. **Decorative Elements**
- **Center Dot**: 3px circle representing focal point
- **Accent Dots**: 2px circles at bottom corners for balance
- **Divider Line**: 2px horizontal line separating brand identity

3. **Professional**: Teal/blue colors signal healthcare and trust
4. **Scalable**: SVG format means perfect clarity at any size

   - Tooth = Health, cleanliness, precision

## 📏 USAGE GUIDELINES

### ✅ CORRECT USAGE
- Always maintain minimum 20px clear space
- Keep aspect ratio locked
- Use full color version on white/light backgrounds
- Use white version on dark backgrounds
- Maintain legibility at all sizes

### ❌ AVOID
- Do not distort or stretch the logo
- Do not change colors without approval
- Do not add effects (shadows, glows) beyond spec
- Do not use on busy/conflicting backgrounds
- Do not reduce below 64px width
- Do not rotate or flip
